## Owner resolution rules

1. Reuse existing canonical financial facts and S1 evidence.
2. Calculate normalized after-tax operating earnings (NOPAT), using evidence-supported adjustments only.
3. Calculate invested capital using a denominator consistent with the earnings scope and measurement period.
4. Evaluate historical ROIC and its sustainability.
5. Compare normalized ROIC with an appropriately supported cost-of-capital estimate.
6. Assess incremental capital efficiency using available historical information.
7. Do not require exact project-level or factory-level attribution unless the approved metric contract explicitly makes it mandatory.
8. Where exact values cannot be established, use supported ranges or N/R according to the approved scoring policy.
9. Reflect unresolved uncertainty through data confidence and rubric evidence, without automatically assigning zero or midpoint scores.
10. Do not treat missing optional analytical detail as a mandatory scoring blocker.

## Acceptance

S1 may close only with traceable normalized earnings methodology/inputs, consistent capital, reproducible ROIC/economic-return assessment, documented material adjustments/assumptions, mandatory M3 evidence satisfied and no unresolved material economic contradiction. If an indispensable input is unavailable, keep BLOCKING with its exact identifier, source requirement and reason.

Weights, rubric definitions, source authority, M1 and production permissions are unchanged. Closure requires verified evidence and successful relevant validation. Approval of this resolution approach does not itself close S1 or approve calculated observations.
