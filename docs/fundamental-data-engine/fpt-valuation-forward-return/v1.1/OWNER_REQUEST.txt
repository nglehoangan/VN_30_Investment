## 16. No-Rerating Shareholder Return Assessment

Implement the approved quantitative no-rerating form.

The economic objective remains canonical VAL-RET.

### 16.1 Ordinary return model

Calculate five-year shareholder IRR from:

- Initial purchase price.
- Future shareholder cash distributions.
- Terminal sale proceeds.

Preserve shareholding-entitlement adjustments.

### 16.2 Scale-free zero-credit model

When:

- No future dividends are credited.
- Entry and terminal valuation multiples are equal.
- The starting and ending earnings definitions are economically comparable.
- Growth and dilution are properly aligned.

The return identity is:

IRR = (1 + aggregate earnings growth) / (1 + economic dilution) − 1.

For a relative terminal multiple factor m over five years:

IRR = [(1 + growth) / (1 + dilution)] × m^(1/5) − 1.

Where:

- m = 1 represents no rerating.
- m below 1 represents multiple contraction.

Support annual variable growth and dilution paths where appropriate rather than requiring constant rates.

### 16.3 Eligibility

An exact normalized EPS point estimate is not mandatory for the scale-free form when:

- A comparable normalized economic earnings basis is established.
- Starting economic foundation remains disclosed.
- Economic growth and dilution assumptions are qualified.
- Entry and terminal valuation bases are comparable.
- Multiple-contraction risk is assessed.
- Current price and share basis are eligible.

Do not waive the normalized economic foundation requirement.

Do not treat mathematical cancellation as proof of earnings sustainability.

Do not treat no rerating as a prediction that the market cannot derate.

### 16.4 Contraction scenarios

Evaluate:

- No rerating.
- Supported moderate multiple contraction.
- Supported adverse contraction.
