## 9. Reasonableness Review framework

Use the six approved review dimensions.

### RV01 — Historical Consistency

Review the estimate against available historical financial and operating evidence.

Historical similarity is not mandatory.

A different future trajectory may be accepted when the analyst explains the economic reason.

### RV02 — External Corroboration

OPTIONAL SUPPORT.

Use retained broker, industry and macro evidence when relevant.

Do not require a second analyst organization to provide the same estimate.

Do not require an external document to confirm the precise chosen growth rate, fair multiple, dividend, dilution or terminal forecast.

Absence of exact external corroboration is not an independent blocking condition.

### RV03 — Economic Consistency

MANDATORY REVIEW.

Assess whether the assumptions are economically plausible considering:

- Business model.
- Demand.
- Competitive position.
- Returns on capital.
- Reinvestment.
- Cash generation.
- Capital requirements.
- Funding.
- Shareholder economics.

Use sector-appropriate reasoning.

### RV04 — Internal Consistency

MANDATORY REVIEW.

Review consistency among:

- Earnings.
- Growth.
- Reinvestment.
- Cash flow.
- Payout.
- Funding.
- Dilution.
- Valuation.
- Corporate actions.

Reject material contradictions or double counting.

### RV05 — Conservatism and Sensitivity

MANDATORY REVIEW.

Review representative, downside and upside assumptions.

A reasonable base need not remain within one canonical band under every stress scenario.

Do not require an exhaustive mathematically guaranteed bound over all possible future events.

Distinguish a defensible expected-return assessment from a stress-only floor.

### RV06 — Counterevidence

MANDATORY REVIEW.

Review all material adverse evidence in the frozen dataset.

Do not require elimination of every uncertainty.

Distinguish:

- Resolved contradiction.
- Bounded limitation.
- Unresolved non-invalidating uncertainty.
- Material contradiction.
- Confirmed invalidation.

Only economically relevant material contradictions should block the affected conclusion.

## 10. Reviewer independence

Separate analyst proposal and reviewer challenge into distinct persisted steps.

The reviewer must challenge the assumptions rather than simply restate the analyst's rationale.

Record:

- Specific issue.
- Relevant frozen evidence.
- Economic reasoning.
- Potential effect on the model.
- Materiality.
- Recommended correction if required.

The reviewer cannot silently approve unsupported assumptions.

Two reviews performed by the same AI are procedural separation, not independent external expert corroboration.

Do not describe them as independent research organizations.

No additional external evidence discovery is permitted.

## 11. Permitted blocking conditions

Use the following blocking reason classes.

RB01 — Invalid factual basis.

RB02 — Incorrect arithmetic, formula, units or scope.

RB03 — Material economic or internal contradiction.

RB04 — Arbitrary estimate without defensible reasoning.

RB05 — Material uncertainty that prevents a defensible economic conclusion.

Every blocking disposition must identify:

- Specific failed assumption or conclusion.
- Actual affected model and rubric.
- Evidence or contradiction.
- Why the issue is material.
- Whether a simpler approved model could resolve it.
- Why an estimate, range or conservative case cannot reasonably resolve it.

The following are not automatic blockers:

- No precise externally published forecast.
- No forecast extending to the exact terminal date.
- No externally published normalized EPS.
- No second broker agreeing with the estimate.
- No guarantee of future dividend.
- No exact future ESOP schedule.
- Scenario results crossing canonical bands.
- Expected IRR below the investor objective.
- Low confidence by itself, subject to existing governing gates.

Preserve mandatory source and scoring authority requirements.

## 12. Reasonableness Review dispositions

Support:

ACCEPTED.

ACCEPTED_WITH_LIMITATIONS.

REVISION_REQUIRED.

REJECTED.

An ACCEPTED_WITH_LIMITATIONS estimate can support valuation and scoring when the economic conclusion remains defensible.

Map accepted estimates into existing QUALIFIED_ESTIMATE or BOUNDED_ESTIMATE structures with explicit model-specific limitations.

Do not promote an estimate merely because the reviewer assigned an accepted label.

Require the recorded reasoning and evidence lineage.

Do not overwrite source facts with estimates.

Do not promote hypothetical sensitivities without economic justification.
