### Changed executable predicates

- **FH-STRESS.** Existing native topics are `scenario/funding_response/capital_response`, enforced by `calculateScorecard` in `src/domain/current-scoring/scorecard.ts`. The v2 review further required modeled funding response. This proposed version evaluates audited cash, debt/equity, current balance sheet, EBIT/interest, annual CFO/FCF and separately comparable interim CFO/capex. Exact daily timing and undisclosed responses are not inputs to this band. A composite with positive operating support and negative interim FCF maps to 2; adequate buffers map to 3 or 4, weak support maps to 0/1 with observed evidence, and 5 remains reserved for verified severe protection. For FPT FY25 interest coverage 13.57× and current ratio 1.40×, 9T26 FCF −1.077 trillion VND; short debt exceeds book cash. The refinancing risk remains non-compensable.
- **GQ-INC.** Native topics include the denominator and unit economics. Successor uses bounded multi-year productivity, independently supported capital cost, reviewed scope bounds and persistence. Operating capital alternatives are admissible when their defensible range stabilizes against cost. No exact private organic or deal-level attribution. A stable range maps through existing proposed cost-relative boundaries; 4/5 need persistence. FPT's alternative windows cross the bounds and the market/operating-scope comparison is missing.
- **VAL-PRIMARY.** Native checks combine topics with the global action comparability predicate. Successor checks same-cutoff accepted market inputs and its own earning-power and normalization review instead; it does not waive per-share financial comparability. FPT price 50,100 VND and 1,885,727,648 shares are attached from the accepted dataset readback. No same-post-FTEL earning-power range and independent value. The valuation selector compares base and independent estimate within a band and applies the conservative cap; scenario bands need not be identical.
- **CA-REINV.** Native requires capex/acquisitions/divestments and global action comparability. The revised company-level rule consumes matched CFO/capex history and public allocation disclosure. It has no private IRR, project attribution or unrelated corporate-action predicate. FY2025 capex 5.098 trillion VND, FCF decreased from 8.428 to 5.038 trillion, positive over four FYs. Operating cash after investment with conflicting trends selects mixed 1. No 0 or 2 inferred from totals. Extreme outcomes need dedicated evidence and calibration.
- **CA-DIL.** Native terms/proceeds/per-share and action controls are preserved for historical M3. The successor takes verified issuance amounts, economic per-share effects by event, material-event coverage and shareholder-protection evidence. 0 proposed at cumulative loss ≥1%; 1 requires nonnegative effect and support for shareholder protections; an intermediate loss or incomplete evidence returns N/R. Employee-retention value is not mandatory. FPT has the quantity and proceeds; eligible event-price basis and protected cumulative per-share economics are unresolved.

These five replaced requirements were obsolete as evaluation demands in the reviewed drafts. The native scorer enforced required topic bindings and action comparability, not a literal private daily forecast or project IRR condition. Legitimate source/PIT, same-scope normalization, downside evidence and non-compensable financial-risk checks remain. New methods run only in preview. The legacy scorer still enforces V8's two-rubric authorization scope.
