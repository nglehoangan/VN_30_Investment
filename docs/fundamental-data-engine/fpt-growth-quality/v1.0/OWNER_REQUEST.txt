### 6.3 Method A — Public Growth Profile

Economic objective:

Assess the observed quality and direction of growth using qualified public financial evidence.

Evidence requirements:

- Trend: CORE.
- Base effects: CORE.
- Acquisition/disposal review: CONDITIONAL according to materiality.
- Per-share economics: CORE economic dimension, with approved public proxies and band-dependent precision.
- Cash realization: SUPPORTING or CONDITIONAL when it materially changes growth quality.
- Exact internal organic-growth attribution: OPTIONAL unless necessary to establish the selected band.

Permit:

- Revenue CAGR.
- Reported NPAT growth with normalization qualifications.
- Adjusted paired EPS growth.
- Public segment growth.
- Historical margin trends.
- CFO and FCF trends.
- Corporate-action and M&A materiality review.

Do not require exact organic versus acquired growth percentages for every band.

However, do not treat acquisition-driven growth automatically as organic growth.

### 6.4 Method A bands

0 — STRUCTURAL_VALUE_DESTRUCTIVE_CONTRACTION

Evidence supports persistent economic deterioration or contraction associated with value destruction.

Missing data or one weak reporting period alone is insufficient.

1 — WEAK_PUBLIC_GROWTH_PROFILE

Evidence supports weak growth, persistent earnings pressure or deteriorating economic quality.

Distinguish temporary cyclicality from structural weakness.

2 — LIMITED_OR_LOW_QUALITY_GROWTH_PROFILE

Observed growth is limited or its economic quality is weak.

Growth may be heavily dependent on base effects, acquisitions, dilution, temporary margins or weak cash realization.

Require actual evidence of low-quality growth rather than missing normalization alone.

3 — MODERATE_VALUE_CREATING_GROWTH_PROFILE

Evidence supports meaningful positive growth in operating or per-share economic outcomes.

Material limitations may remain concerning organic attribution, cash conversion or comparability.

The observed growth must not be explained solely by mechanical corporate actions, unsupported one-offs or capital issuance.

Do not require complete organic-growth attribution or a fully normalized per-share bridge if the selected public growth profile remains supported.

4 — STRONG_PUBLIC_GROWTH_PROFILE

Multiple compatible public evidence families support strong and persistent growth.

Require meaningful corroboration from earnings, per-share outcomes and economic quality.

Material adverse counterevidence must be resolved or bounded.

5 — Not permitted for Method A.

Method A maximum: 4/5.

### 8.4 Method A — Incremental Capital Productivity Profile

Economic objective:

Assess the observed quality, direction and consistency of incremental capital productivity without claiming an exact normalized incremental ROIC.

Method A is an explicitly approved economic profile assessment.

It is not an exemption from assessing capital deployed.

Required economic evidence:

- Incremental operating earnings.
- Incremental capital deployment.
- Relationship between earnings and capital growth.
- Multi-year productivity trend.
- Material CAPEX and asset movements.
- Material M&A and commissioning effects.
- Relevant independent operating corroboration.
- Cost-of-capital context when asserting economic value creation.

Supporting evidence:

- CFO.
- FCF.
- Segment results.
- Operating margins.
- Asset utilization.
- Observable unit productivity.

Exact project IRR is optional.

Exact private project-level cost attribution is not universally required.

However, material scope differences and economically meaningless capital denominators remain disqualifying where they undermine the selected profile.

### 8.5 Method A bands

0 — EVIDENCED_VALUE_DESTRUCTIVE_EXPANSION

Evidence supports incremental deployment associated with clear economic value destruction.

Require strong evidence of the economic relationship between deployed capital and adverse outcomes.

A single poor annual ratio is insufficient.

1 — PERSISTENTLY_WEAK_CAPITAL_PRODUCTIVITY

Evidence supports systematically weak incremental outcomes relative to capital deployed.

Review multiple comparable windows, deployment timing and independent counterevidence.

Do not classify immature productive investment as persistently weak solely because benefits have not yet appeared.

2 — MIXED_OR_DETERIORATING_CAPITAL_PRODUCTIVITY

Evidence supports a mixed or deteriorating incremental productivity profile.

Relevant evidence may include:

- Lower incremental earnings relative to capital deployment.
- Deterioration across multiple defensible capital classifications.
- Recent capital investment increasing faster than operating results.
- Weak recent incremental cash outcomes.
- Material uncertainty over deployment maturity or attribution.

The assessment does not need to establish an exact canonical incremental ROIC band.

However, missing data, a single unsupported denominator or the mere existence of M&A cannot independently establish this band.

The deterioration or mixed productivity conclusion must be supported by actual economic observations.

3 — ACCEPTABLE_INCREMENTAL_CAPITAL_PRODUCTIVITY

Evidence supports reasonable incremental economic outcomes relative to additional capital deployed.

Require corroboration across relevant windows and independent operating evidence.

Where the conclusion asserts value creation, assess the relevant cost-of-capital range.

Material unresolved evidence that could reverse the productivity conclusion prevents acceptance.

4–5 — Not permitted for Method A.

Method A maximum: 3/5.

### 8.6 Distinguish Method A and PX02 conclusions

PX02 asks whether an aligned incremental-return range supports the canonical economic band.

Method A asks whether an independently supported capital productivity profile exists.

Do not require Method A to prove that the issuer's absolute incremental ROIC is within the canonical PX02 band.

For Method A band 2, unresolved exact incremental ROIC may remain when the mixed or deteriorating productivity conclusion is independently supported.

Do not use Method A to conceal confirmed weak returns, capital destruction or adverse deployment outcomes.

Do not automatically award 2 because PX02 failed.
