## Exact research blockers — 9 rubric chưa có điểm đề xuất

- **FH-STRESS**: Confirm unrestricted cash/deposit realization dates and covenant/acceleration terms; join all maturities, lease payments and capital commitments to the computed operating shock. A positive shocked CFO alone cannot prove survival.
- **GQ-INC**: Attribute recurring after-tax operating earnings and economic capital to the same investment cohorts; resolve shrinking cash-adjusted denominator and lease policy. Two stock balance sheets cannot establish incremental project returns.
- **IC-POS**: Supply comparable dated VNM/competitor market-share definitions or independently corroborated cost/distribution position. Group revenue outgrowth versus a domestic industry statistic is not a share bridge.
- **CA-DIST**: Reconcile FY25 parent dividends 10.136tn against common earnings 8.423tn, prior-year distributions/reserves, tax timing and remaining committed high-return uses. Actual distributions do not themselves prove discipline.
- **CA-DIL**: Complete FY22–25 parent issuance/treasury/rights/convertible terms and use-of-proceeds reconciliation; FY24–25 unchanged shares and no potential dilution at FY25 are evidence, not the full action history.
- **CA-PSV**: Build FY22–25 fund-adjusted/action-adjusted common-owner earnings, distributions and economic capital per share; test real cycle-adjusted intrinsic compounding. FY24–25 EPS growth of 0.14% alone is insufficient.
- **VAL-PRIMARY**: Adopt normalized parent common cash and assumptions; qualify share/action/treasury/rights bridge and TCBS price adjustment at explicit market cutoff; reconcile independent crosscheck and post-FY25 changes.
- **VAL-RET**: Resolve VAL-PRIMARY dependencies, then adopt five-year distribution, dilution and exit cases/probabilities. Conditional IRRs below are not accepted expected return.
- **VAL-MOS**: Resolve VAL-PRIMARY plus unrestricted liquidity/downside obligations. Conservative case/price discount remains conditional; do not equate low P/E with downside protection.
