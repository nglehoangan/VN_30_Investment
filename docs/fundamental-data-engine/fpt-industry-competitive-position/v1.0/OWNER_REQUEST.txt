## 6. IC-IND — Industry Structure

### 6.1 Economic objective

Assess whether the industry's economic structure supports durable value creation.

This is an industry-level assessment, not an issuer-quality assessment.

Preserve maximum 4 points.

Existing canonical topics:

- barriers.
- competition.
- demand.
- disruption.

### 6.2 Method B — Direct Industry Assessment

Preserve existing direct methodology.

Evaluate:

- Barriers to entry.
- Competitive intensity.
- Pricing discipline.
- Customer bargaining power.
- Supplier bargaining power.
- Capital intensity.
- Industry profitability.
- Regulatory structure.
- Structural demand.
- Substitution and disruption.

Use comparable public industry evidence, official statistics, regulation, competitor disclosures and applicable industry-economic observations.

Paid industry research is not mandatory.

Preserve canonical bands:

| Points | Canonical band |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Value-destructive industry |
| 1 | Structurally difficult |
| 2 | Mixed/cyclical/average |
| 3 | Generally attractive |
| 4 | Structurally attractive/rational/durable |

Maximum 4/4 requires industry-level economic strength, rational competition and durable demand.

Being the strongest company in a weak industry does not establish a high industry score.

### 6.3 Method A — Public Industry Structure Profile

Economic objective:

Assess the observable economic profile of the relevant industry using qualified public evidence.

Required CORE dimensions:

- Entry barriers.
- Competitive intensity.
- Structural demand.
- Disruption and substitution.

Conditional dimensions:

- Customer bargaining power.
- Supplier bargaining power.
- Regulation.
- Capital intensity.
- Industry profitability.

Conditional evidence becomes required when material to the selected band.

Method A must include relevant evidence external to the assessed issuer.

Suitable external evidence may include:

- Official industry data.
- Independent public research.
- Regulatory disclosures.
- Competitor annual reports.
- Public market-demand studies.
- Publicly observable market entry and capacity changes.

Do not require complete market-wide profitability data for every band.

However, do not infer industry economics from FPT operating margins, ROIC or revenue growth alone.

### 6.4 Method A bands

0 — VALUE_DESTRUCTIVE_INDUSTRY_PROFILE

Evidence establishes persistent industry-wide economic deterioration or value destruction.

Require evidence beyond an isolated issuer loss or a single difficult period.

Missing industry data is insufficient.

1 — STRUCTURALLY_DIFFICULT_INDUSTRY_PROFILE

Evidence supports sustained competitive pressure, weak bargaining economics, substitution risk or other structural impediments to value creation.

Distinguish structural difficulty from temporary cyclicality.

2 — MIXED_INDUSTRY_STRUCTURE_PROFILE

Relevant industry evidence supports both meaningful economic opportunities and material competitive, cyclical or disruption pressures.

The observed profile must support the mixed conclusion.

Do not assign 2 automatically when profitability data is unavailable.

3 — ATTRACTIVE_PUBLIC_INDUSTRY_PROFILE

Independent public evidence supports durable demand, meaningful barriers and relatively manageable competitive pressure.

Require stronger industry-economic corroboration than band 2.

Known material adverse structural evidence must be resolved or bounded.

4 — Not permitted for Method A.

Method A maximum: 3/4.

### 7.4 Method A — Competitive Capability Profile

Economic objective:

Assess demonstrated competitive capability using public commercial and operating evidence.

Method A does not certify a numerical market share, relative cost leadership, top-tier market rank or overall market leadership.

Required economic dimensions:

- Market participation.
- Actual commercial wins or equivalent observable customer outcomes.
- Delivery and execution capability.
- Customer access and distribution.
- Operating economics.

Supporting and band-dependent evidence:

- Relative scale.
- Peer observations.
- Independent competitive corroboration.
- Market share.
- Relative cost.
- Product or service differentiation.

Exact market share is not mandatory for all bands.

Exact competitor unit costs are not mandatory for all bands.

Do not require Method A to prove canonical PX03 relative-market-position semantics.

However, require affirmative evidence of actual competitive capability.

Do not assign points solely from:

- Revenue scale.
- Employee count.
- Revenue growth.
- Absolute profit.
- Issuer claims of leadership.
- One isolated contract.
- The existing BQ-MOAT score.

### 7.5 Method A bands

0 — STRUCTURALLY_DISADVANTAGED_CAPABILITY_PROFILE

Evidence establishes a structural competitive disadvantage that materially impairs the issuer's ability to win, serve or retain market participation.

Missing comparator data alone is insufficient.

Require affirmative adverse economic evidence.

1 — WEAK_COMPETITIVE_CAPABILITY_PROFILE

Evidence supports weak competitive capability, persistent commercial deterioration, poor execution, loss of customer access or unfavorable operating outcomes.

Distinguish temporary weakness from a more persistent competitive problem.

2 — ESTABLISHED_COMPETITIVE_CAPABILITY_PROFILE

Evidence establishes actual competitive participation and demonstrated ability to win and serve customers.

Require corroborated evidence covering:

- Commercial outcomes.
- Delivery capability.
- Market access.
- Operating economics.

Exact market share and relative unit cost may remain unresolved.

Do not claim market leadership or top-tier relative positioning.

Do not assign this band merely because the issuer remains operational.

3 — STRONG_COMPETITIVE_CAPABILITY_PROFILE

Multiple independent evidence families support strong competitive capability.

Require:

- Repeated commercial wins.
- Demonstrated execution at relevant scale.
- Established customer access.
- Strong operating outcomes.
- Meaningful independent corroboration of relative capability or competitive strength in the defined segment.

An exact numerical market rank is not mandatory.

However, issuer scale and internal margin alone cannot establish this band.

4 — Not permitted for Method A.

Method A maximum: 3/4.

### 7.6 Distinguish PX03 and Method A

PX03 asks whether the issuer's relative market position is supported by aligned comparative evidence.

Method A asks whether the issuer's competitive capability is supported by observable commercial and operating outcomes.

Do not require Method A band 2 to establish an exact canonical market-rank or cost-position conclusion.

Conversely:

- Do not allow Method A to override a valid adverse relative-market assessment.
- Do not ignore evidence of competitive losses.
- Do not convert unsupported leadership claims into strong capability.
- Do not mechanically assign 2 whenever PX03 fails.

## 9. IC-CYCLE — Industry Cycle Position

### 9.1 Economic objective

- NOT CYCLICAL / N.A.

NOT CYCLICAL / N.A. does not automatically produce 1 or 2 points.

### 9.2 Method B — Detailed Cycle Assessment

Preserve existing detailed cycle methodology.

Evaluate:

- Industry demand and supply.
- Capacity and utilization where relevant.
- Order and spending cycles.
- Normalized earnings.
- Normalized margins.
- Downside sensitivity.
- Recovery potential.
- Financial and operating survivability.
- Forward economic asymmetry.

Preserve canonical bands:

| Points | Canonical band |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Adverse normalized forward asymmetry |
| 1 | Balanced normalized forward asymmetry |
| 2 | Favorable normalized forward asymmetry |

Maximum 2/2 requires affirmative evidence of favorable normalized forward asymmetry.

Do not award 2/2 solely because:

- Current earnings are high.
- Revenue is growing.
- Contracts are increasing.
- Share price is rising.
- Valuation is low.
- The industry appears depressed.

A depressed industry with weak survivability is not automatically attractive.

### 9.3 Method A — Cycle Exposure Profile

Economic objective:

Assess the observed economic opportunities and vulnerabilities associated with the relevant industry cycle.

Required evidence dimensions:

- Relevant industry demand or cycle signals.
- Comparable operating trends.
- Earnings or margin normalization context.
- Forward cycle-related demand risks.
- Financial and operating vulnerability.

Conditional evidence:

- Capacity utilization.
- Industry supply changes.
- Commodity price curves.
- Customer spending patterns.
- Detailed funding response.
- Capital-response measures.

Method A does not require formal certification of survival under every adverse scenario.

However, it must explicitly review financial limitations that could materially change the cycle-exposure conclusion.

Do not interpret existing FH Method A scores as verified survivability.

### 9.4 Method A bands

0 — ADVERSE_CYCLE_EXPOSURE

Evidence establishes material adverse economic exposure to the relevant cycle or structural deterioration.

Examples may include:

- Unsustainable peak profitability.
- Demand contraction.
- Material excess supply.
- Deteriorating normalized economics.
- Meaningful vulnerability to the expected industry downturn.

Require affirmative cycle-related evidence.

Missing cycle data is insufficient.

1 — MIXED_OR_BALANCED_CYCLE_EXPOSURE

Evidence supports meaningful positive and negative cycle-related economic signals.

The selected profile may retain uncertainty about exact cycle phase or normalized forward asymmetry.

The analyst must explain:

- What economic cycle or demand pattern is relevant.
- Which observations demonstrate potential upside.
- Which observations demonstrate downside exposure.
- Why the conclusion is mixed rather than clearly adverse.
- Why favorable normalized forward asymmetry has not been established.

Do not assign 1 automatically to non-cyclical businesses.

Do not assign 1 solely because a definitive cycle classification is unavailable.

2 — Not permitted for Method A.

Method A maximum: 1/2.

### Evidence-specific rules

- Primary home: IC-POS.
- BQ-MOAT use requires a separate supported mechanism or persistence conclusion.

- Primary home: BQ-MOAT.

- Primary home: GQ-FWD.

- IC-POS may use actual competitive wins, not unsupported future conversion.

- Primary home: IC-IND.

- Primary home: FH-STRESS.

- Do not award another resilience score for the same economic claim.

Market price and valuation discount:

- Primary home: Valuation.
- Do not use either to create additional IC-CYCLE credit.
