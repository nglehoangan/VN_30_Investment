## 5. Proposed assessment structure

Evaluate four evidence groups.

A. Dilution Magnitude

B. Incentive Alignment

C. Shareholder Protection

D. Observed Economic Outcome

Do not introduce numeric sub-scores.

Do not introduce equal weighting or automatic checklist scoring.

Analyst must provide a coherent economic conclusion.

Reviewer must assess the conclusion and material counterevidence.

## 6. Group A — Dilution Magnitude

Use actual FY2022–FY2026 share programme observations.

Calculate:

- Annual programme size relative to relevant share bases.
- Actual ownership dilution where event denominator is established.
- Economic transfer sensitivity.
- Cumulative dilution on comparable bases.
- Actual proceeds and expense limitations.
- Discount to event-aligned economic value references.

Distinguish new shares, treasury shares and pro-rata distributions.

Do not treat period opening shares automatically as exact event pre-issue shares.

Review recurrence, changes in programme scale and adverse economics.

## 11. Proposed research band semantics

Maintain binary weight.

Proposed positive candidate:

1/1 — DISCIPLINED_WITH_LIMITATIONS.

Requires a defensible holistic conclusion based on all relevant evidence groups, controlled issuance economics, reasonable programme purpose, substantive protections and supporting observed economic evidence.

The analyst must address the material economic transfer and recurrence.

The absence of proven harm alone does not qualify the positive band.

Proposed adverse candidate:

0/1 — MATERIAL_VALUE_DESTRUCTION_OR_WEAK_ECONOMIC_DISCIPLINE.

Requires affirmative evidence of materially harmful issuance or materially weak economic discipline to the detriment of existing shareholders.

Missing evidence alone cannot justify zero.

N/R:

Neither band is defensible after consideration of available evidence and material counterevidence.

Do not label the new positive candidate as verified neutral/accretive economics.

Preserve existing canonical CA-DIL semantics in the approved engine.
