## Method independence and owner decision

**Method B** is the comprehensive assessment. It checks timed liquidity coverage and covenant disposition for LIQ and a developed funding and capital-response assessment for STRESS. **Current Method A** runs after B fails for eligible detail gaps and assesses a separate documented public-data case. It accepts qualified book-cash proxies and conservative ranges. It has no exact deposit-access, obligor-matching, precise covenant-headroom or signed refinancing requirement. It still reviews restrictions, covenants and adverse evidence, excludes unsupported funding, preserves obligations and requires the actual band conclusion. LIQ 2 requires qualified adequacy *with* material refinancing dependence. STRESS 2 requires evidence of severe vulnerability proportionate to that band. A detailed positive capital response is unnecessary for STRESS 2, while survival bands require affirmative support. Material uncertainties may remain when they do not undermine the band. Known adverse evidence cannot be overridden. [Approved owner methodology](OWNER_REQUEST.txt#L103) · [Fallback contract](../../../../src/domain/current-scoring/fh-dual-route.ts#L30)

**Alternative Method A** would make the analyst's reasoning explicit through qualified signals, evidence-backed ranges, counterevidence, confidence and economic limitations. It can already receive an existing numeric band under v1.1 if it establishes that band independently of unsupported assumptions. The validator consumes that judgment and its documentation. It does not derive the conclusion from the ratios or stress output. The four cases are **A** (candidate judgment insufficiently supported in the submitted assessment) and **F** (the retained record leaves the band-defining conclusion unresolved). **B** is unsupported as a cause because the judgment condition matches the approved economic requirement. **C** is unsupported because the B detail failures expressly opened A. **D** is unsupported because no qualified band was submitted or rejected. **E** is unsupported because the input, route diagnostics and Effective Scores agree through replay and the workflow. [Approved route and ceilings](../../../03%20SCORING/WP2_FINANCIAL_HEALTH_DUAL_ROUTE_v1.1.json) · [Band and evidence predicates](../../../../src/domain/current-scoring/fh-dual-route.ts#L43)

An amendment would be needed if the owner wants these proxy signals to earn 2 points **while the canonical economic conclusion remains unresolved**. The exact decision is whether to keep the canonical conclusion and approve specific evidence-backed assumptions or bounds sufficient to establish it, or to redefine band 2 as a proxy-based risk classification. For LIQ, specify the usable-resource and rollover conditions that can establish adequacy. For STRESS, specify when stressed cash deficits and maturity concentration establish severe vulnerability despite uncertainty over buffers and necessary outlays. Either policy change should specify what contrary evidence prevents the band and how a proxy-based result may feed Effective Score. No such change is needed for a qualified assessment within the current contract.
