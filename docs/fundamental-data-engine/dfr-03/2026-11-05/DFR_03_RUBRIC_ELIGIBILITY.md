# DFR-03 rubric eligibility

RESEARCH PROVISIONAL · Review 2026-11-05 · Financial values/periods/cutoff unchanged from DFR-02. No total score, ranking or production acceptance.

READY_FOR_ASSESSMENT means all topic evidence is sufficient for the bounded research judgment; completed rows remain in that eligibility class with a separate completion status. DATA_MISSING means required *qualified topic evidence* is absent from the bound layer, not that a physical PDF is absent. Inspect retained notes before seeking new evidence or asking the owner for files. ANALYST_REVIEW_PENDING means related economic material exists but topic sufficiency/band/challenge work is unfinished. MATERIAL_CONFLICT requires an actual material contradictory dependency; a 1-VND EPS discrepancy does not automatically qualify. NOT_APPLICABLE requires explicit approved-methodology permission; no instance is declared N/A and no weight is redistributed.

The four existing judgments remain provisional and are revalidated, not relabelled as owner-approved or formally accepted. Preparation/challenge are separate actual invocations and distinct self-review passes by the same assistant. Independent human or authenticated model/session attestation is not claimed.
