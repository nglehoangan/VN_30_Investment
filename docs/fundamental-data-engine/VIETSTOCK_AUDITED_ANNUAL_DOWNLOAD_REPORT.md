## Interpretation

A download is not a completed fundamental-data review. Scanned PDFs may rely on explicit provider scope labels. Archive attachments and original/corrected editions are preserved without asserting which edition should prevail; the manifest records individual hashes and provenance. Provider update timestamps are not asserted as authoritative publication boundaries.
