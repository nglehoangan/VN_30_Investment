## 5. Implement incremental assessment lifecycle

Support the following semantic update states using existing compatible schemas where possible.

### BASELINE

The initial qualified fundamental assessment.

### UPDATED

New evidence supports a newly calculated or reassessed band.

The score may increase, decrease or remain unchanged.

### REAFFIRMED

New evidence has been reviewed and independently supports the existing economic conclusion.

### CARRIED_FORWARD

The latest valid assessment is inherited because current information is insufficient for a complete new calculation, but the previous economic conclusion remains defensible after a materiality and freshness review.

### INVALIDATED

The previous assessment is no longer economically reliable because of material changes, expired evidence, unresolved contradictions or incompatible economic scope.

If an invalidated assessment cannot be replaced through the authorized assessment routes, the current scoring result is N/R.

Update status must remain separate from:

- VERIFIED / CONSERVATIVE / N/R.
- Confidence.
- Score validity.
- Production authorization.

Do not represent an inherited assessment as a newly calculated current-period return metric.

## 6. Incremental decision rules

Apply the following decision sequence:

1. Locate the latest qualified BQ-EQ assessment.
2. Verify its original evidence, methodology, economic scope and lineage.
3. Review eligible new financial information and material events.
4. Determine whether those developments affect the previous economic conclusion.
5. Attempt a consistent current-period assessment where sufficient information exists.
6. If a new assessment is supported, use UPDATED or REAFFIRMED.
7. If a complete new assessment cannot be constructed, explicitly test whether the previous assessment remains economically valid.
8. If inheritance is defensible, preserve the previous score using CARRIED_FORWARD.
9. Reassess confidence and disclose current-period limitations.
10. If inheritance is not defensible, apply the authorized D1–D8 fallback routes.
11. Assign N/R only when neither a valid inherited assessment nor a defensible new assessment is available.

Do not require a complete recalculation of current ROIC as a prerequisite for inheritance.

Do not interpret the absence of negative evidence alone as proof that the old assessment remains valid.

An unresolved material change that prevents a defensible conclusion about the applicability of the previous assessment must not be concealed through inheritance.

## 7. Materiality review

Evaluate changes in:

- Core business activities.
- Operating profitability.
- Economic returns.
- Capital structure.
- Financial leverage.
- Business segment composition.
- Consolidation scope.
- Acquisitions and divestments.
- Accounting treatment.
- Cost of capital.
- Material adverse developments.

Classify each relevant change as:

- NO_MATERIAL_IMPACT
- MATERIAL_BUT_BOUNDED
- MATERIAL_INVALIDATING
- MATERIAL_IMPACT_UNRESOLVED

A structural or accounting change must not automatically invalidate the previous economic assessment.

Determine whether the change materially affects the economic conclusion, not merely the availability of a particular accounting ratio.

Inheritance may be permitted for MATERIAL_BUT_BOUNDED when supported evidence establishes that the previous band remains economically defensible.

Do not inherit when MATERIAL_INVALIDATING or MATERIAL_IMPACT_UNRESOLVED prevents a reliable conclusion.

Do not introduce arbitrary numerical materiality thresholds without approved authority.
