# CODEX TASK ADDENDUM — BQ-EQ v1.2 Effective Score & Downstream Scoring Integration

## 1. Owner direction

The owner clarifies that preserving and displaying the Latest Valid Assessment is insufficient.

The latest valid score must also be usable by subsequent analytical scoring processes when a new reporting period does not provide sufficient information to establish a replacement assessment.

Extend the approved BQ-EQ v1.2 work package with D19–D23.

- D19: Introduce Effective Score semantics based on the latest qualified assessment when a new assessment cannot be established.
- D20: Consume Effective Score in category aggregation and provisional Investment Score calculation.
- D21: Make Effective Score available to comparative analysis, research ranking and Investment Decision inputs.
- D22: MATERIAL_IMPACT_UNRESOLVED and missing affirmative support must not automatically block provisional Effective Score.
- D23: Preserve explicit distinctions between confirmed current scores, inherited provisional scores, evidence freshness, coverage, ranking eligibility and actionability.

This addendum supersedes any previous instruction that categorically prohibits the use of pending historical assessments in downstream analytical score aggregation.

It does not authorize bypassing Stage 0, Risk Policy, hard veto, evidence integrity, production authorization or execution controls.

## 3. Effective Score contract

Implement or reuse three separate semantic values:

1. Latest Valid Score.
2. Current Reassessment Score.
3. Effective Score.

Effective Score is the numeric assessment used for downstream analytical scoring.

Apply the following precedence:

A. If a valid current reassessment exists, use its points.

B. If a valid current inherited or reaffirmed assessment exists, use its points.

C. If the current reassessment is incomplete, retain the latest qualified assessment as a provisional Effective Score, provided that:

- Its original economic assessment was valid.
- Its evidence integrity and identity remain established.
- Its applicable freshness requirements have not expired.
- No confirmed material invalidation has occurred.

D. If the historical assessment has been materially invalidated or expired, do not use it as Effective Score.

E. If no qualified assessment exists, Effective Score remains null.

Never assign zero or an arbitrary midpoint because information is missing.

Do not require a complete recalculation of current ROIC merely to use a provisional Effective Score.

## 4. Correct materiality semantics

Preserve MATERIAL_IMPACT_UNRESOLVED as a meaningful uncertainty status.

It must:

- Trigger MATERIAL_REVIEW_PENDING.
- Preserve the latest valid historical assessment.
- Permit provisional Effective Score where the conditions in Section 3 are satisfied.
- Record the unresolved economic dependency.
- Affect confidence and downstream actionability as applicable.
- Prevent unsupported certification of the historical score as a newly confirmed current assessment.

It must not:

- Automatically invalidate the historical assessment.
- Automatically remove Effective Score.
- Automatically remove analytical scoring coverage.

MATERIAL_INVALIDATING must continue to prevent use of the invalidated assessment for current Effective Score.

Do not remove material counterevidence checks from replacement assessment validation.

Retain affirmative support requirements for confirmed current inheritance.

Do not require affirmative new evidence solely to use a qualified provisional Effective Score.
