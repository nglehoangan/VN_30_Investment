PHASE 2 — ACCOUNTING SEMANTICS
==================================================

Define candidate metric:

REPORTED_BORROWINGS

Suggested semantics:

Issuer-reported consolidated borrowing principal consisting of:

- short-term borrowings;
- current portion of long-term borrowings counted once;
- non-current borrowings;
- separately disclosed bonds/debt securities;
- separately disclosed finance-lease financing liabilities if the approved
  scoring definition includes them;
- separately disclosed other interest-bearing financing principal.

Exclude:

- trade payables;
- customer advances;
- taxes;
- wages;
- ordinary accrued operating expenses;
- provisions;
- dividends payable;
- deferred revenue;
- miscellaneous liabilities unless issuer identifies them as financing /
  interest-bearing.

Crucially:

Do NOT require proof that every unspecified operating/miscellaneous payable
is non-interest-bearing before REPORTED_BORROWINGS can be admitted.

Unknown miscellaneous liabilities remain separately flagged as uncertainty.
