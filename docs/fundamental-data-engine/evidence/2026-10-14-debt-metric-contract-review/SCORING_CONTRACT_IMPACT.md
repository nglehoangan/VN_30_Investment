# M3 scoring contract impact

A reported/comprehensive taxonomy amendment is recommended, not activated. The approved intent is interest-bearing leverage and solvency/funding resilience, not certified absence of financing within every unnamed payable. Reported quantities plus explicit financing additions can support that intent when measurement, lease consistency, cash and risk qualifications are satisfied. This changes the evidence-population contract, not leverage score anchors or investment thresholds.

The current manual-current/hybrid path requires FINANCIAL_DEBT and mechanically derives NET_DEBT by subtracting CASH_AND_EQUIVALENTS. Generic M3 calculation can consume NET_DEBT/normalized EBITDA. Approved FH-BS-01 and Net Debt definitions require unrestricted cash; the current generic cash subtraction does not prove cash eligibility. Debt/equity and net-debt/equity are documented contextual metrics, not currently implemented calculator IDs; Debt/assets is not established as an active formula. Interest coverage does not consume a debt stock numerically. See M3_DEPENDENCY_ANALYSIS.json for precise executable/documented distinctions.

A later migration must implement a basis-aware debt selection and Net Debt bridge, distinct derived identity or version, unrestricted-cash evidence, denominator normalization and sector lease comparability. Current HPG EBITDA/normalized interest/cash eligibility and supporting definitions are not established by this review; no ratio, category score or financial-health approval is generated. Real-estate/conglomerate liabilities, presales/customer funding, commitments and liquidity remain distinct risk exposures even if they are excluded from a reported borrowing numerator.
