## Tolerances and interpretation

Provider-internal accounting identities use absolute 1,000 raw monetary units or relative 0.000001 (1ppm), based on the mean operand magnitude. Comparisons remain provisional while units are unknown. EPS Detail/Summary comparison uses an absolute 0.01, not the monetary 1,000 tolerance. Exact decimal/BigInt arithmetic is used. Conflicts quarantine affected candidates. Balance closing cash is compared to CF closing cash only when the mapped concept, raw provider type and date agree; bank cash/metal is not equated with CF cash equivalents.

DIRECT means observed code/template/number/window availability under the POC mapping. It does **not** mean independently validated units/scope/calendar, approved accounting authority, production canonical acceptance, M3 readiness or scoring eligibility. All 7,050 normalized provider facts and 4,234 canonical candidates have productionAdmissible=false. No provider accounting observation entered the approved manual dataset.
