# Document authenticity checklist

2026-10-28 · PREPARATION ONLY · NO REAL EVIDENCE INTAKE OR ACCEPTANCE

No checklist item is marked complete for a real document. Automation can detect schema/hash/time/duplicate inconsistencies, but it cannot create issuer authenticity, account ownership or authorized independent acceptance.

**1. Issuer attribution**

Automated preparation: Record provider/document namespace; check schema and byte provenance.

Required authenticated review: Verify actual issuer-controlled origin or genuine signature/correspondence through independently established authorized channels; letterhead/hash alone insufficient.

**2. Original document identity**

Automated preparation: Check document ID/reference, original byte length/hash, source variant and pointers.

Required authenticated review: Privately confirm original issue/reference and relation to requested snapshot; distinguish redacted derivative/reissue.

**3. Account ownership**

Automated preparation: Check required private reference fields and consistent account/perimeter aliases.

Required authenticated review: Compare full original private identifiers and ownership/authorized-use evidence; masked number cannot suffice.

**4. Exact position references**

Automated preparation: Check all 11 requested rows and original reference pointers are present/unique.

Required authenticated review: Confirm exact original statement/snapshot row-to-instrument relation, not current balance/ticker coincidence.

**5. Instrument identifiers**

Automated preparation: Compare code/ISIN syntax, namespace/venue/class, duplicates and conflicts.

Required authenticated review: Verify account-specific stable instrument relation and accepted alternate evidence if ISIN absent; do not infer it.

**6. Effective dates**

Automated preparation: Check explicit precision, interval order, coverage gaps and honest source/retrieval/knowledge times.

Required authenticated review: Confirm witnessed applicability and exceptions; no current snapshot or listing/retrieval date substituted for historical continuity.

**7. Corporate-action references**

Automated preparation: Check action/allocation/entitlement IDs and partition references are present, with pending issues explicit.

Required authenticated review: Confirm issuer/account stage, ordinary/pending/credit/trading evidence; missing FPT dates remain UNKNOWN.

**8. Document integrity**

Automated preparation: Recompute original byte hash/size through a future bounded authorized read; check derivative hash separately.

Required authenticated review: Confirm retained original and custody chain; integrity does not authenticate issuer or financial content.

**9. Supersession status**

Automated preparation: Check predecessor/version IDs and simultaneous conflicting sources.

Required authenticated review: Confirm actual issuer revision/correction scope; no latest-wins or overwrite of old evidence.

**10. Revocation status**

Automated preparation: Check required current decision/source revocation references and fail on unknown head.

Required authenticated review: Authorized reviewer checks genuine source/decision/key/role validity under separately approved trust infrastructure.

A broker support response or app login may support source provenance but is not an independent acceptance role in this application. Reviewers must be genuinely producer-distinct, authorized and authenticated by a separately approved mechanism. Do not invent a provider, reviewer name, signature or verified role. If verification channels/authority are absent, source authenticity/readiness remain BLOCKED even when the file hash matches. No rejection, revocation or acceptance event is created in this phase.
