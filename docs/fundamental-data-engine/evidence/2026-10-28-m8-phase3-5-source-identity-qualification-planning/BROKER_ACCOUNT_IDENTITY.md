# Portfolio and broker-account identity

2026-10-28 · SOURCE_IDENTITY_QUALIFICATION_PLANNING_ONLY · DESIGN, NOT ACCEPTANCE

Proposed BrokerAccountBindingV1 fields: exact portfolioID/ledger namespace; broker legal provider/product namespace; private exact account/subaccount/custody identifiers; ownership or delegated authority witness; economic perimeter/currency and exclusions; original source/document/request identity; explicit effective interval; original document hash/row/page pointers; knowledge/recorded times; scoped authenticated acceptance reference; revocation/supersession. This is a proposed data contract, not an instantiated account mapping.

Private verification must compare full original source account identifiers and ownership evidence against the intended owner-authorized portfolio. Resolve broker trading account versus custody/subaccount/product differences explicitly. AccountNo/custodyID are different concepts until source contract proves their relation. Masks, lastfour, similar owner names, tickers, balances, OSusername, folder paths and APIlogin possession are insufficient. Exclude other accounts, bond/margin/derivative products or subaccounts only with explicit perimeter evidence; unresolved economic obligations cannot disappear by an arbitrary narrowed perimeter.

Proposed secure process: owner obtains original broker-issued package via existing broker secure channel; retain original unmodified privately with random opaque evidence IDs, restricted access and backups. Authorized independent reviewer inspects exact identifiers and ownership/source authenticity privately. A future approved verifier binds full private evidence bytes/hash and scope to an opaque account alias in a public/redacted summary. Redact names/IDs from distributable documents, paths, URLs/querystrings, source fields, error logs and traces. Store redacted derivative separately with its own hash and redaction provenance; never overwrite original bytes. A document hash/alias is not proof of exact match until that private review occurs.

Do not publish a plain hash of an account number, a guessable salt/lastfour, or credentials. Do not commission encryption/signature/key infrastructure in this task. A future trust design must establish authorized access, key/role revocation, audit and reviewer identity; no-login environment currently supplies none of that authenticated acceptance. OWNER_ACCOUNT_A here is a redacted planning alias, not an assigned authenticated identity or accepted account binding.

Minimum private evidence: official ownership/account identification + statement/addendum naming exact original opening snapshot/account/position scope, applicable dates and issuer-verifiable document reference. One certified package can cover ownership, account/perimeter and all11instrument anchors if explicit. If owner cannot obtain it, retain UNRESOLVED and block qualification; masked APIUI data cannot substitute.
