# Current NAV component contract

2026-10-27 · Phase 3.1 DESIGN PROPOSAL ONLY. No calculator, adapter, schema validator, snapshot store, migration, acceptance artifact or Phase 3.2 implementation is created. Proposed amendments require explicit owner review.

Canonical formula: **NAV = settled cash + disjoint securities market value + disjoint receivables − disjoint liabilities**. All authoritative amounts are VND exact decimal strings. Zero needs complete-scope evidence; missing/null/unknown never becomes zero. Reconstructed ledger is accounting truth; broker observations corroborate and expose missing events, never overwrite it.

Corporate actions require accepted replay/identity/price-basis alignment; no implicit successor or ticker substitution.

Proposed D3 refinement distinguishes **TRADE_UNSETTLED_ORDINARY** from **CORPORATE_ACTION_PENDING_RIGHT**. A verified executed BUY already recognized as ordinary economic ownership under M2 is valued at its qualified ordinary close with its exact unpaid tradePAYABLE; lower availableTrading alone is not another asset or loss of ownership. SELL removes quantity and creates its net receivable on trade date. Settlement swaps cash/obligation without another quantity/value/income effect. This does not authorize trading unavailable shares. By contrast, a stock-dividend/rights claim such as unresolved FPT_WFT cannot be priced as ordinary solely because totals resemble ordinary stock. Unknown legal rights/quantity/CA basis still blocks.

An availability flag must never override the accepted economic-unit classification or existing execution gates.
