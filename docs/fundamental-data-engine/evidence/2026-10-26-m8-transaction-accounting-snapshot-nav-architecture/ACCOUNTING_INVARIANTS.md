# Accounting invariants

1. Immutable facts + matching ordered legs, exact decimal12 half-even MWAC, deterministic asOf replay and source watermark remain authority. Never independently edit holdings, cash, basis, snapshots or P&L.
2. BUY/SELL are internal portfolio transformations, not external investor contributions. Bank auto-funding is external only for a declared broker-portfolio perimeter excluding the bank account; if bank is inside the perimeter it is an internal transfer. Fix perimeter before XIRR.
3. Contributions/withdrawals use approved CASH_DEPOSIT/CASH_WITHDRAWAL classification. Opening assets are neither contribution nor gain; income/dividends/interest are not external capital. Trade settlement only clears its linked obligation and cannot realize P&L or quantity again.
4. Gross/net/split representation must reconcile fees/taxes exactly once. Capitalized BUY charges enter MWAC; SELL released cost and net proceeds derive realized P&L. Broker displayed cost and P/L are reconciliation observations, not a replacement rounding/cost policy.
5. Ordinary DIVIDEND_CASH reflects approved actual receipt; net-only evidence leaves gross/tax analytics unavailable. Imported receivables require **explicit linked claim clearing** with no duplicate income. Existing DIVIDEND_CASH/TRADE_SETTLEMENT cannot currently do arbitrary opening-claim clearing; D4 proposal is prerequisite where needed. Never use CASH_ADJUSTMENT as a balancing shortcut.
6. Entitlement credit and pending→ordinary transfer are separate events; transferring a pending bucket has zero net economic share addition after entitlement recognition. No label-only alias, inferred ratio, listing/date guess or implicit corporate successor. Pending quantity is not spendable/tradable ordinary quantity.
7. Cash/quantity/obligation invariant checks, no-margin funding and downstream affected-transition validation remain exact. Do not spend an unsettled receivable or ignore an unresolved obligation of105 because it looks small; approved exact reconciliation has no discretionary tolerance.
8. Current NAV = qualified assets + cash + nonduplicated claims − nonduplicated liabilities at one defined valuation instant/perimeter. A missing component is UNKNOWN, not default0 evidence; default serialized zeros in old models do not prove completeness. Trade prices never replace market close; no stale/future fallback.
9. No synthetic daily NAV. Snapshots are derived audit records of observed valued states, never new opening truth. Correction preserves original and regenerates from corrected upstream evidence/version.
10. XIRR uses investor-signed external flows and valid terminal value; trading cash/dividend receipts are not separately investor distributions unless actually withdrawn out of perimeter. No daily NAV requirement; no double-counting terminal sale proceeds plus the same terminal assets.
11. Realized P&L is supported-history MWAC result, unrealized P&L needs current price/basis. Total change requires a valid start/end NAV and interval capital flows; do not call incomplete imported gains lifetime or investment-return CAGR.
12. Source purpose, publisher/identity, knowledge cutoff, independent acceptance, owner scope, revocation and exact reconciliation remain separate gates. Technical hashes, Codex results and architecture direction cannot grant them. Unknown drawdown is never NORMAL and a NAV snapshot never activates scoring.
