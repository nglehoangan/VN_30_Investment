# Proposed lifecycle — review only

| Layer | What it proves/freezes | What expires or requires requalification | What it does not authorize |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Durable Data Initialization | Certified effective universe, identity/sector masters, qualified accounting/history, source provenance, Level1 disposition/PolicyB, ACTIVE TCX exception, approved policies and tested market capability | A new membership/source/schema/policy/correction may require a new version or incremental initialization; old acceptance stays reproducible as-of its original cutoff | Current market usability, complete M3 score, ranking or capital |
| B. Market Data Readiness | Approved source and transport/manual-ingestion contract, normalization, raw retention, time/identity/adjustments, freshness evaluator, deterministic replay | Credential/provider/schema/calendar/contract changes require requalification; availability monitoring is separate from historical capability proof | A proof that today's market snapshot exists or is fresh |
| C. Analysis-time Market Refresh | Request coverage, trusted analysis cutoff, accepted immutable raw-backed observations and contemporaneous eligibility proof | New required session, expiry, source revision, corporate action or dependency mismatch blocks reuse | DecisionReady by itself; qualitative/valuation/risk gates still apply |
| D. Decision Snapshot | Universe/fundamental/market/portfolio versions, policy/model/engine versions, input hashes, freshness proof and owner exceptions at decision time | New live action requires new eligibility/currentness checks; frozen historical decision remains replayable | Trading on old evidence merely because a past decision was accepted |

**Data Initialization → Market Data Refresh → Analysis → Portfolio Snapshot → Decision → Decision Snapshot.**

This is the logical order. A decision is a candidate until the final decision envelope is sealed atomically with publication; a failed seal must not leave an actionable unbound output. Portfolio snapshot uses the frozen market batch (including held but not ranking-eligible names) and a current ledger watermark. If ledger/reference/market context changes between stages, block and rebuild the affected context; never mutate ledger accounting during market refresh.

The existing implementation already contains pieces of this separation: current read model checks `validThrough < now`; evidence validation checks run as-of; snapshot and decision bodies retain partial lineage. It does not yet expose a separate durable initialization certificate plus accepted first-class market snapshot contract. A dated old acceptance does not literally expire in the existing immutable snapshot code merely because wall-clock time advances. The current release has failed acceptance, not a formerly accepted DI certificate automatically revoked by natural aging.

Legacy ranking currently requires all30 scorecards. An approved prospective PolicyB-aware route must represent full30 identities plus eligible29 and explicit MBB exclusion; a fresh market snapshot alone does not fix that compatibility boundary. Do not invent a score for MBB or silently shrink the legacy route.

The candidate formula needs changes: AnalysisReady also requires all existing applicable financial-cycle, qualitative, valuation, confidence and model gates. DecisionReady additionally requires portfolio integrity/currentness, M1/M4/M5 risk and permission gates. Market readiness is necessary for price-dependent outputs, not sufficient for a recommendation. Fundamental-only descriptive calculations may be explicitly scoped separately; they must not masquerade as complete scores/rankings or redistribute missing weights.

Fundamental partition stays FULL30, eligible by fundamentals29, DEFERRED MBB. A holding in MBB may still need a market price for NAV/risk; that does not cure MBB's CAR blocker or allow new capital. Full-universe membership and ranking eligibility are distinct from the request's observation coverage. Flow/technical remains NOT_APPLICABLE_PERMANENT_M3; a separate technical/money-flow request requires its own approved workflow and gate, never new M3 weight.
