# MARKET_DATA_FRESHNESS_GATE — proposed contract

Apply before valuation, any price-dependent scoring component, ranking/Top10, positive-capital recommendation and any separately approved technical/money-flow analysis. It is a required gate in those workflows, not a dashboard badge. M3's permanent flow/technical exclusion and weights stay unchanged.

Inputs: trusted requestId/purpose/mode and analysisAsOf/knownAt; initialization certificate; effective universe/sector and PolicyB disposition hashes; explicit required-observation/coverage plan; approved market capability/source/freshness/calendar/adjustment versions; immutable marketSnapshotId; exact source/observation/lineage hashes; prerequisite qualitative/valuation/model evidence.

1. Derive the use-case coverage and required session/field/basis from approved policy and trusted clock. Full VN30 ranking must account for all eligible29 and retain MBB as excluded, not silently shrink the denominator. Portfolio valuation includes all held positions even if some are not eligible for ranking/new capital. Declare benchmark/FX inputs when required.
2. Validate initialization certificate/current applicable master and financial-cycle requirements, active exceptions and quarantine. Initialization alone is not analysis permission.
3. Load exact immutable market data and source bytes; validate authority, identity, declared units, time, field/adjustment semantics, conflicts/revisions, coverage and transformation lineage. A hash or normalized number alone is not source evidence.
4. Recompute freshness at the trusted consumption cutoff using the preapproved session/calendar policy. `CURRENT_ANALYSIS` may not supply a past asOf to evade current clock checks. Retrieval today does not make an old trading date current. If a cached snapshot remains eligible and coherent it can be reused; otherwise acquire/validate through the approved refresh path. Refresh failure leaves the gate blocked.
5. Return an immutable gate proof pinning request, purpose, cutoff, input-set hash, observation hash set, policy/calendar versions, eligibleUntil and per-input reasons. Eligibility cannot exceed the earliest mandatory dependency expiry. Status is PASS or BLOCKED with typed reasons; no score0 or neutral fill.
6. Scoring/valuation/ranking consumers assert the exact proof matches their actual data, not a caller-supplied PASS boolean. All existing human/qualitative, valuation, confidence and methodology gates still apply. Check again before publishing an actionable output, DCA/new capital or handing it to execution. Expiry, ledger/reference/source revision or corporate-action mismatch invalidates the live authorization.

Cache key must include purpose/coverage hash, mode/asOf, effective universe, source/schema, calendar/freshness/adjustment policy and source revisions. A cache hit never bypasses recomputation of eligibility. Do not fall back from the latest invalid/unknown quote to an older valid quote silently. No fixed-day allowance is added by this proposal.

For the Oct19→Oct25 example, assume the earlier snapshot is stale under the approved trading-session policy. Initialization remains an auditable historical PASS if actually approved; the new request is BLOCKED until a fresh accepted snapshot exists. On nontrading dates the required session follows the approved calendar, not necessarily the calendar date of the request. Hypothetical dates do not establish a universal six-day threshold.

Historical/backtest mode never invokes a current refresh to fill old gaps. Require historically effective universe/sector, historically available fundamentals/estimates, historical market observations and revision/adjustment/owner-policy knowledge at simulated cutoff. CURRENT_ANALYSIS qualification, later publication, restatements or a later owner exception cannot retroactively create PIT knowledge. Replaying an old decision evaluates its frozen then-current freshness proof, while new live reuse evaluates today's eligibility.

Existing hard-risk alerts/exits must not be hidden by a missing market feed. This proposal grants no execution bypass: price-dependent sizing/execution and any special risk procedure remain subject to their existing M1/M4/M5 contracts. It cannot authorize new capital under stale/missing data.
