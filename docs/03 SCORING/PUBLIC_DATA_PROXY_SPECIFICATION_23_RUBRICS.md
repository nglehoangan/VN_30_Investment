**Date:** 2026-11-25

**Status:** Approved Baseline v1.0 · methodology authority `m3-public-data-proxy-v1.0.0` · [owner receipt](PUBLIC_DATA_PROXY_OWNER_APPROVAL.json)

This specification implements the design brief arising from the [25 November feasibility audit](PUBLIC_DATA_FEASIBILITY_AUDIT_23_RUBRICS.md). It preserves the [23 canonical rubrics, bands and maximum prerequisites](../../src/domain/scoring/methodology.ts#L4) and all seven category weights, totaling 100. Five rubrics have newly approved public-data routes. Eighteen have existing methodological routes, comprising five direct, five existing proxy and eight analyst-model routes. Actual issuer-level N/R remains available in every class. No generic rubric was assigned N_R_RETAINED because the retained cases do not establish that every authorized route fails for that rubric. Existing methodology permission and permission to adopt a particular issuer assessment remain separate.

## Evidence and score rules

- Build from retained admitted data. Prioritize audited figures and notes, annual and interim reports, AGM resolutions and governance reports, issuer event notices, official industry data and traded prices. Prefer original audited figures when scopes conflict. Use matched fiscal horizons, revision, consolidation, attribution, units and availability. Record issuer, document, page/table, effective and publication dates, knowledge clock, admission scope and each formula operand. Retrospective current-purpose evidence needs its own binding before historical PIT use.
- **VERIFIED** means a reviewed existing-band assessment with all required topics, usable references, a coherent period/scope and material counterevidence resolved. Estimated inputs stay labeled as estimates. **CONSERVATIVE** requires a complete eligible assessment, the lowest defensible applicable existing band and its minimum point, explicit opposing evidence and review. A defensible representative band must survive the material uncertainty relevant to its claim; defer to N/R when the evidence cannot establish any defensible band. Native EBCS checks an already scored row and cannot waive missing topics. [Native scoring](../../src/domain/current-scoring/scorecard.ts#L101) · [EBCS preview](../../src/domain/current-scoring/evidence-based-preview.ts#L27)
- **N/R** applies when a required topic has neither direct evidence nor an authorized, defensible substitute, or when counterevidence, period/scope, material exposure or model instability prevents a band. Missing disclosure never establishes zero activity, zero risk or a negative economic result. Maximum needs explicit prerequisites, exceptional justification and reviewed disconfirming evidence. Upper endpoints of multi-point bands need stronger support. Separate Stage 0, veto, residual-risk, score authority and publication gates apply.
- Each repeated evidence family needs a distinct economic channel. In particular, use issuer economics for moat, shock behavior for resilience, peer rank for competitive position, payment timing for liquidity, survival for stress, incremental productivity for growth, management allocation outcomes for reinvestment, and net ownership effects for dilution. Preserve issuer payout versus investor cash boundaries. Current dependency and expected-return gates apply. [Action dependencies](../../src/domain/current-scoring/action-dependencies.ts#L5) · [Metric definitions](METRIC_DEFINITIONS.md) · [Sector normalization](SECTOR_NORMALIZATION.md)
- Availability shorthand **P** PRIVATE_OR_UNAVAILABLE, **V** VOLUNTARY_PUBLIC, **T** EXTERNAL_PUBLIC_PAID, **U** LEGAL_DISCLOSURE_UNVERIFIED. U concerns the obligation for the exact field, with the prior audit's legal findings carried forward. R required topic, X substitutable evidence, S supporting, O optional. An input listed as conditional becomes critical only when the selected method depends on it or it can materially move the band. FY windows below are evidence design guidance. Apply existing longer history or exact period rules where relevant.

## Priority evidence definitions and existing-band mapping

The mappings describe evidence combinations that can support a band subject to the shared VERIFIED, CONSERVATIVE and N/R conditions above. They are qualitative comparisons against existing anchors. No new numeric cutoffs, interpolation or issuer scores are introduced.

### BQ-MOAT · Existing proxy

Bind **advantage** to a named and observable mechanism, **economic_behavior** to its independently supported result, and **persistence** to recurrence across at least three comparable FYs or the relevant competitive period. Use segment revenue and gross or operating profit, disclosed volumes, comparable channel footprint, publicly priced repeat contracts, bank deposit mix and funding cost, or documented switching features. Compare gross profit/revenue and same-product revenue/volume only where the mix is reconcilable. For banks, compare normalized funding cost on matched average funding and control for rate/tenor, service costs and credit. For technology use contract tenure and renewals where actually disclosed. Check adverse price competition, incentives, product mix, channel loss and weak periods. No estimate of undisclosed churn or CAC is necessary.

**Bands:** 0 evidenced commodity or disadvantage; 1 isolated weak advantage; 2 narrow advantage with modest recurring effect; 3 identified differentiation supported by observed economics; 4 meaningful effect with a credible erosion channel; 5 strong multi-period advantage across adverse observations; 6 multiple independently supported durable mechanisms. Unexplained margin alone supports no band. Rate narrower claims conservatively where mechanism and persistence still stand; retain N/R if the mechanism or attribution is unbounded.

### BQ-RES · Existing proxy

Bind **concentration** using consolidated segment, geography or channel revenue and identified material concentration disclosures. Calculate the relevant category's revenue/total revenue on a matched basis. This measures that axis only. A broad group line cannot cap exposure to an unidentified customer within it unless the customer's exposure is confined to a demonstrably bounded population. Bind **pricing_power** using same-product volume, realized unit price, margin and disclosed cost/mix changes. Bind **cyclicality/stress** using performance and financing during a documented adverse period or supported scenario. Use at least three FYs with comparable scope plus a downturn, shock or independently grounded sensitivity. For banks review deposit and loan concentrations, sector/collateral mix and credit stress; for property review project/geography, collections and legal timing; for retail/technology review category, channel, geography or contract mix as relevant.

**Bands:** 0 demonstrated structural fragility; 1 severe unmitigated dependency; 2 material vulnerabilities despite partial mitigation; 3 evidenced mixed exposures and shock response; 4 diversified with strong measured shock behavior; 5 severe-stress franchise protection without distressed capital actions. Review price/mix confounding, top-customer uncertainty and shocks outside the observed period. An unresolved material axis or uncertain pricing/stress attribution capable of moving the band requires N/R. Distinguish commercial shock response from FH-STRESS funding survival.

### BQ-CASH · Existing proxy

For at least three audited FYs plus matching interim/YTD periods, extract consolidated CFO, common-owner or otherwise aligned NPAT, cash paid for PP&E/intangibles, receivable/inventory/payable movements, noncash items and one-off disclosures. **Realization** uses CFO/normalized matched NPAT where meaningful and CFO less total cash capex. **One_offs, working_capital, recognition** require a signed bridge distinguishing timing, provisioning, disposals and accounting policy. Do not equate asset additions with cash capex or interpret total capex as maintenance. For banks use recurring earnings, migration, coverage, provisions and recognition in lieu of industrial CFO/FCF. For property compare advances, receivables, collections, deliveries and matched recognition over the project cycle.

**Bands:** 0 persistent failure to turn reported economics into cash/realization; 1–2 weak results with recurring adverse adjustments; 3–4 adequate results with material qualifications; 5–6 strong recurring realization and sound recognition despite adverse-period checks. Upper endpoints require stronger evidence. Six requires transparent adjustments and reconciliation, not a standalone conversion ratio. Test factoring, supplier financing, capex deferral, inventory release and provisioning cycles. N/R if matching scope or recurring realization cannot be judged. Use the same cash series in valuation only as a separately identified valuation operand.

### FH-LIQ · PX01 approved covenant and availability binding

Extract obligations by disclosed due bucket, interest, bond/lease maturities, restricted/pledged cash, deposit maturity, documented facilities, disclosed covenant/triggers and breaches, collateral and published funding events. From the latest qualified period through cutoff, bridge every relevant bucket, including 12/24 months when relevant and a short-horizon bank stress bucket where supported. **Available resources minus cumulative obligations** = unrestricted cash + dated realizable inflows after review/haircuts + proven drawable facilities − dated cumulative outflows. Deduct funding needed to release collateral, and count cash, collateral and facility capacity only once. BANK uses behavioral deposit outflow and matched contractual funding, dated high-quality assets and confirmed monetization. Property presales count only when timing, legal condition and availability support the inflow. Use historical funding behavior only as evidence for a bounded assumption. 

Proposed **covenants** binding: a reviewed issuer/event inventory with one disposition per material instrument: terms evidenced; supported inapplicability; or unresolved terms with conservative resources and demonstrated immaterial effect across scenarios. Exact private covenant headroom remains material where compliance affects a necessary facility or acceleration. Unknown terms are neither a waiver nor proof of no covenant.

**Bands:** 0 observed shortfall without credible funding after reasonable alternatives; 1 severe funding dependence; 2 covered only with material refinancing dependence; 3 sound timed coverage and manageable refinancing; 4 resilient timing, diversified credible funding and well-supported covenant protection. Test delayed cash, collateral overlap, runoffs, covenant-triggered acceleration and facility expiry. N/R when essential cash timing or financing can flip coverage. No 4 from aggregate book cash or undisclosed terms.

### FH-STRESS · Existing analyst model

Use latest comparable balance sheet and funding disclosures plus multi-year adverse-period income/CFO references. For industrial/retail/technology, stress revenues, margins, working capital, capex commitments and refinancing costs. For property include collection/handover delay, project legal constraints and debt maturities. For BANK stress NIM, credit migration/cost, deposits and RWA; bridge eligible starting capital through modeled earnings, distributions, capital deductions and stressed RWA on matched scope. Project cash opening plus stressed operating inflows less operating, capex and debt outflows plus independently defensible financing. Keep funding response and capital response as separate required outputs. Treat unsupported facilities as unavailable for the scenario and test the significance of that assumption.

**Bands:** 0 evidenced failure under plausible stress; 1 likely distressed financing; 2 severe vulnerability; 3 survival with material constraints; 4 resilience with manageable constraints; 5 supported severe survival without harmful refinancing, emergency dilution or distressed disposal. Review counterevidence from overlapping funding, collateral, acceleration and correlated shocks. N/R if a necessary funding response or capital bridge remains indeterminate. Public buffers alone are supporting evidence under existing M3.

### GQ-FWD · Existing analyst model

Use at least three FYs and matched latest interim results for segment sales/volumes, operating margin, normalized cash conversion, capex, capacity, obligations and net funding. Add official sector demand, disclosed order or pre-sale conversion and execution of previous AGM plans when comparable. Model revenue as supported volume × price or addressable demand × a justified achievable share. Reconcile margin, investment, operating cash and funded capacity. BANK separately models earning-asset growth, NIM, credit cost, retained capital and RWA. Property requires phased lawful delivery and collection. Retail/technology use separately evidenced footprint or contract/segment productivity. Forecast 3–5 years with joint adverse and representative assumptions; assess **runway, reinvestment, funding, execution** independently.

**Bands:** 0 demonstrated structural decline; 1 weak visibility/stagnation supported by the economics; 2 constrained or cyclical runway; 3 credible moderate trajectory; 4 good funded runway with demonstrated execution and manageable range; 5 attractive high-confidence multi-year runway with independent corroboration and funding. Check prior guidance misses, capacity/credit/working-capital demand, expiry/renewal and sensitivity to unsupported conversion. N/R where assumptions span incompatible outcomes or no coherent funding/operating case exists.

### GQ-INC · PX02 approved issuer-level productivity binding

Use at least three comparable FYs, multiple positive capital-deployment windows, beginning/end invested capital, normalized EBIT/tax or bank common earnings/equity, cash deployment, goodwill and acquisition/disposal scope, tangible operating assets and economically material leases. Derive a range of **Δ normalized NOPAT / Δ invested capital** across defensible windows and capital definitions aligned to cash deployment and commissioning. Compare to the separately supported cost-of-capital range. For banks use Δ normalized common earnings / Δ aligned common equity or supported marginal risk capital, adjusting for credit, funding and capital cost. Do not divide by a near-zero or unexplained negative denominator. For property align cash investment and delivery lags; for retail keep store cohorts where footprint mix can change the answer. Proposed `unit_economics` binding is a reviewed, persistent issuer-level productivity bridge where no economically representative unit exists or can be observed, with explicit scope bounds and independently supported operating observations. A one-year ratio cannot bind persistence.

**Bands:** 0 repeated value destruction after adjustments; 1 weak returns below economic cost; 2 mediocre economics with weak spread or adverse durability; 3 acceptable aligned economics; 4 strong surplus supported across windows; 5 exceptional durable spread and robust denominator, including sector-specific prerequisites. Check one-off EBIT, acquisitions, impairment, delay, leverage and denominator sensitivity. N/R if window/definition differences produce inconsistent bands without a defensible conservative conclusion. This rewards return on incremental capital. CA-REINV separately reviews the management decision and outcome.

### IC-POS · PX03 approved relative position binding

Fix the market or peer population, product, geography, period and comparison basis before reviewing rank. Prefer issuer same-scope sales/volume divided by official relevant totals; otherwise propose an explicitly labeled **listed-peer rank** from comparable audited sales/volume or an independently observable public position indicator. Also corroborate **cost_position** with like-for-like unit costs or adjusted operating margin/productivity, and **distribution** with compatible outlet/branch coverage, capacity or documented channels. Repeat over at least three comparable FYs or a justified relevant market window. BANK compare deposits/loans and funding cost on the same segments; technology use vertical or geography where actually disclosed; retail use category/channel and compatible outlet metrics. Market rank and peer rank measure different populations and must be reported accordingly.

**Bands:** 0 evidenced disadvantage; 1 weak relative position; 2 competitive observable rank and economics; 3 strong top-tier position corroborated across share/efficiency/channel; 4 sustainable clear leadership on a representative defined market with independent confirmation. Sample choice, weak comparator scope, subsidized channels and mix-correlated margins are required challenges. N/R where comparator markets are incompatible or either cost/distribution lacks supported evidence. Never infer market leadership from restricted peer-set rank. Separate market position from BQ-MOAT's mechanism and persistence.

### RG-RISK · Existing analyst model

Use latest FY/interim risk notes, public material contracts/events, debt/collateral, FX or hedge disclosures, counterparty exposures, guarantee terms, and adverse historical outcomes. By risk channel, bound gross exposure, apply shocked economic effect, subtract only the independently evidenced effective mitigant on matching magnitude, maturity, seniority and counterparty. Use bank credit, funding, market and operational scopes appropriate to regulatory capital; property adds legal and delivery exposure. Check effectiveness under correlated shocks and avoid overlap between insurance, guarantee, cash collateral and operational mitigants. Evidence of no mitigant or ineffective mitigation can support an adverse band when exposure is established.

**Bands:** 0 HIGH supported material residual exposure; 1 ELEVATED_WEAK with inadequate controls; 2 ELEVATED_CONTROLLED with evidenced but incomplete control; 3 MODERATE with bounded exposure and tested mitigants; 4 LOW with independently corroborated limited material residual exposure. Must match external risk state. The UNACCEPTABLE veto remains separate. N/R where an unknown term or exposure could move the residual category and no defensible lower band is supported.

### CA-REINV · PX04 approved consolidated outcome binding

Extract CFO, paid cash capex, asset/CWIP movements and commissioning, acquisition/disposal cash and consideration, goodwill/impairments, observable segment outcomes and related AGM intentions. Use at least three audited FYs plus each material event from announcement through a meaningful post-deployment outcome period. Compute CFO less cash capex as context, separately bridge M&A/disposals and relate management commitments to observed capacity, margin, cash or write-offs. Eliminate internal transfers and PP&E commissioning duplicates. BANK uses capital deployed into lending/businesses or acquisitions, funding/credit-adjusted contribution and risk capital; industrial CAPEX/FCF is not a bank substitute. Proposed binding requires event-specific evidence or qualified review of event immateriality, and action comparability only if the scope/event/per-share economics depend on it.

**Bands:** 0 repeated evidenced value destruction, such as failed deployment and impairment; 1 mixed/acceptable outcomes backed by cash and operating evidence with bounded attribution; 2 consistently accretive deployment with corroborated outcomes through the cycle, challenge of counterexamples and discipline distinct from GQ-INC. Positive consolidated FCF alone supplies no band. N/R where an immature/material investment, acquisition, disposition or shift in scope makes attribution indeterminate.

### CA-DIL · PX05 approved event-value and offset bounds

Cover material issuance events over the existing review window, through cutoff, from resolution to completion and subsequent reconciliation. Extract before/after common shares, new shares, exercise/subscription terms, economic proceeds net of fees, rights/transfer restrictions, compensation disclosures, actual use of funds and shareholder approvals. Use verified event-aligned raw market quotes as a sensitivity anchor or supported independent economic value, correcting the comparison for rights eligibility, other effective actions and material event information. Illustrative modeled post-issue value per share = (pre-issue equity value + net incremental proceeds + separately substantiated incremental net benefits) / post-issue economically equivalent shares. Compare with the pre-event per-share basis and explicitly value transferable rights where relevant. Include disclosed compensation and other costs once. Track sequential events without summing incompatible percentage bases. For BANK test capital-raising purpose and dilution separately; stronger regulatory ratios do not by themselves demonstrate accretion.

**Bands:** 0 evidence supports material value-destructive dilution across defensible counterevidence; 1 evidence supports disciplined neutral/accretive terms and shareholder protections, with assumption and counterevidence review at maximum. Test alternative pre-event values, use of proceeds, shareholder rights and plausible upside from benefits. Unknown ESOP benefits get no positive credit in the conservative case. If plausible offsets change the conclusion and cannot be bounded, retain N/R. Mechanical splits/bonuses are comparability adjustments. Award 1 for verified absence of material economic dilution over the applicable window only when the reviewed event inventory, discipline, shareholder-protection and maximum-score prerequisites are all satisfied. Reconcile opening and closing shares, equity movements, resolutions, completed issues and effective mechanical actions. Silence alone never proves absence. Use N/R for unresolved coverage or economic effect. No new materiality percentage or intermediate band is introduced.

## Evidence-discovery workflow and stopping rule

1. Resolve one ticker, effective sector, cutoff and scoring purpose. Inspect admitted evidence, current consumer bindings, metrics and retained assessments first. Distinguish missing mapping from missing source.
2. Reuse sufficient direct public evidence. When a granular input is inaccessible or non-mandatory, use a sufficient authorized proxy immediately. Stop searching for the original. Construct forecasts, valuations, stress and risk outputs internally from approved models and admitted public operands.
3. Keep supporting evidence out of native metric requests unless it is needed and qualified. Use existing USE, PRINCIPLE_EQUIVALENT, N_R or NOT_APPLICABLE dispositions. Preserve actual critical-gap and complete-total rules.
4. If no route supports a band, perform at most one bounded evidence-improvement round within authorized scope. Close as N/R when material uncertainty persists. Record an exact reopening trigger and never infer zero or award conservative points by default.
5. Record rubric, route, rounds, whether original-input discovery continued, whether it continued after sufficient evidence, economic reason and N/R reopening trigger. The isolated candidate workflow can validate this record using `validatePublicRouteReview`. The review and source attestations must be included in the eventual pinned adoption packet, with admissibility and independent challenge through existing gates.

This document is the common methodology reference for all five scoring skills. The canonical rubric registry continues to own IDs, weights, bands and maximum prerequisites.
