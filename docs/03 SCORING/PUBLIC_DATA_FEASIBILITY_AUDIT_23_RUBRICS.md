## Public disclosure and timing

- **Listed-company financial reports.** Circular 96/2020/TT-BTC Arts. 10–11 and 14–15 establish periodic and event-driven disclosure. Use audited annual statements including audit opinions, annual reports, reviewed half-year and quarterly statements for listed issuers, applicable governance and meeting disclosures, and disclosures tied to covered capital and material events. Art. 10 annual financial statements are due within ten days of the audit signature and within 90 days of financial year-end. Art. 14 provides interim deadlines and longer deadlines in specified consolidation cases. Review the applicable exemption and current text for a specific issuer/period. The amendments include the applicable English-disclosure schedule and publication procedure changes. These documents support basic accounting and governance inputs. Granular customer metrics, forecasts, project-level economics and nonmaterial deal attribution have no universal disclosure obligation verified in this review. [Circular 96](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/default.aspx?docid=201902&pageid=27160) · [Circular 68/2024](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/default.aspx?docid=211243&pageid=27160) · [Circular 18/2025](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?classid=1&docid=213548&pageid=27160)
- **Banks.** Check issuer-period regime before comparing regulatory capital data. Circular 41/2016 as amended by 22/2023 and Circular 14/2025, effective 15 September 2025, provide regime-specific capital disclosures. Appendix V of the latter defines capital disclosure content. Treat applicable published ratios as mandatory in their applicable scope, not same-cutoff daily data. Exact bank-by-bank transitional adoption, tier fields and cadence are **UNVERIFIED** here. NPL and coverage can be derived from consistently scoped loan-classification and allowance notes when present. Under Circular 22/2019 as amended, including 25/2026, a prudential liquidity limit/reporting obligation alone does not establish public issuer-level availability. Exact public liquidity headroom, internal stress outputs and nonpublic maturity ladders remain **UNVERIFIED** as disclosure obligations. [Circular 41](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/default.aspx?docid=188256&pageid=27160) · [Circular 22/2023](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?classid=1&docid=209488&pageid=27160) · [Circular 14/2025](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?docid=214640&pageid=27160) · [Circular 25/2026](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?classid=1&docid=218533&orggroupid=4&pageid=27160)
- **Property.** Article 6 of the Law on Real Estate Business and Article 4 of Decree 96/2024 govern public information on property/projects put into business. Use applicable event/current project disclosures alongside audited notes. A universal project IRR, exact collection calendar or complete portfolio-level refinance waterfall obligation is **UNVERIFIED**. [Decree 96/2024](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?classid=1&docid=210798&pageid=27160&typegroupid=4)
- **Common clock.** Use latest eligible quarterly/interim data and audited FY anchors, matched fiscal windows, consolidation and revision. Use multi-year data for persistence/cycle, event dates for actions, same-cutoff valid raw price for current valuation and date-bounded industry/peer evidence. For historical use, require actual public availability and admission as of the governing knowledge clock. Product mix, granular counterparties, covenant detail, internal forecasts and exact competitor cost are field-specific, so a required public document does not guarantee the desired granularity. Current unresolved legal field-level duties beyond the cited provisions are **UNVERIFIED**.

## Shared scoring conditions and feasibility matrix

**Access** — M = MANDATORY_PUBLIC under applicable document/event duty, V = VOLUNTARY_PUBLIC where published, F = EXTERNAL_PUBLIC_FREE, T = EXTERNAL_PUBLIC_PAID, P = PRIVATE_OR_UNAVAILABLE, D = DERIVED, E = ANALYST_ESTIMATE (including reasoned judgments), U = legal duty UNVERIFIED. **Criticality** — R = REQUIRED, X = SUBSTITUTABLE within the approved design and exact evidence review, S = SUPPORTING, O = OPTIONAL, NA = NOT_APPLICABLE. Combined inputs show how a required topic is formed. Alternatives describe existing methodological routes subject to approval of the exact assessment. Issues identify ambiguity where the executable has no specific alternative binding.

- **Eligibility and evidence.** `ScoreInput` and `Assessment` require an exact subject and purpose, versioned method, qualified membership and sector, time, source-linked evidence, metric/assessment records, Stage 0, hard veto, residual risk, five confidence dimensions, thesis and double-count review. Each rated rubric needs an explicit assessment, usable reference(s) on every topic, valid disconfirming references if supplied, matching sector and timely assessment. A fact, an estimate and an assumption are distinct evidence classes. The consumer binds admitted data and exact reviewed artifact. The runtime checks labels, bounds and reference usability. Economic sufficiency of a reference or flag is an analyst/reviewer responsibility. [ScoreInput and calculateScorecard](../../src/domain/current-scoring/scorecard.ts#L21) · [validateEvidence](../../src/domain/scoring/evidence.ts#L85)
- **Metric calculations.** Each requested metric binds two ordered operands, units, comparable intervals, normalization and evidence, with optional 3/5-year growth basis and separate operand context. CAGR requires positive matched annual periods and comparable scope/revisions. EPS CAGR needs action-adjusted evidence. Cyclical as-reported metrics can yield null. Banking rejects industrial CFO/FCF, debt/EBITDA, interest cover and ROIC formulas. Activated debt rules require qualified consolidated debt, unrestricted cash and normalized/downside EBITDA for the applicable metric. Missing/divergent inputs result in null or validation rejection, depending on the predicate. Merely listing a metric in M3 creates no request. [calculateMetrics](../../src/domain/scoring/metrics.ts#L61) · [Debt Metric Contract v2](DEBT_METRIC_CONTRACT_v2.md)
- **Band selection and maximum.** Each registry band below is an analyst judgment using its required evidence. Multiple-point bands require stronger evidence at the upper endpoint. Maximum requires an affirmative prerequisite flag, exceptional rationale and usable disconfirming evidence. VAL-RET also requires a model output, supported assumption basis, qualifying confidence/uncertainty flags and exact 8%, 12%, 15%, 18% and 22% bands. Upper-band points require supported confidence and downside; aggressive expansion precludes 8. RG-RISK must match external residual-risk classification. Shared evidence families require distinct economic channels. [calculateScorecard lines 94–128](../../src/domain/current-scoring/scorecard.ts#L94)
- **N/R and conservative scoring.** Missing assessment/topic, unusable counterevidence, unresolved dependency on action comparability or missing expected-return output produces null for the affected rubric. Missing critical facts, any null metric request, any N/R, listed critical gaps or failed double-count review null the canonical total. No redistribution or inferred zero. Stage 0, veto/risk and confidence determine validity/actionability independently. A conservative row uses the lowest supported applicable band with explicit opposing evidence review; gaps that materially change the band remain N/R. Limited ticker releases report rated weight, coverage and limits, and carry separate permissions. [M3 §§9–12](SCORING_ENGINE.md#L639) · [runtime lines 129–153](../../src/domain/current-scoring/scorecard.ts#L129) · [EBCS lines 34–87](../../src/domain/current-scoring/evidence-based-preview.ts#L34)
- **Corporate actions.** An action flag and usable linked evidence are required for GQ-HIST, three VAL rows and four CA rows. Additional rows can inherit the gate through action-sensitive metrics or share/per-share evidence, subject to IC-IND/RG-GOV exception for the latter. Approved adjusted accounting economics require a comparable basis, distinct from substituting adjusted market prices. [requiresActionComparability](../../src/domain/current-scoring/action-dependencies.ts#L5)
